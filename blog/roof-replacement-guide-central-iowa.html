Your roof is the first line of defense against everything Iowa weather throws at your home. From summer hail to winter ice dams, Central Iowa roofs take a beating year after year. But how do you know when it is time to stop patching and start replacing?

We have replaced over 200 roofs across Ankeny, Des Moines, Johnston, and the surrounding metro. Here are the seven signs we see most often. and a realistic look at what a roof replacement costs in 2026.

The 7 Warning Signs Your Roof Needs Replaced

1. Your Roof Is Over 20 Years Old

Most asphalt shingle roofs in Iowa last between 20 and 30 years. If your roof is approaching that age range and you are starting to see other issues on this list, it is probably time to start planning for a replacement rather than sinking money into repairs.

2. Shingles Are Curling, Cracking, or Missing

Take a look at your roof from the ground. If shingles are curling at the edges, cracking down the middle, or missing altogether, your roof is losing its ability to keep water out. A few missing shingles after a storm can be repaired. But if the problem is widespread, a full replacement makes more sense.

3. Granules Are Piling Up in Your Gutters

Those tiny granules on your shingles are not just for looks. they protect the asphalt from UV rays and weather. When they start washing off and collecting in your gutters, your shingles are breaking down. This is one of the earliest signs of a roof reaching the end of its life.

4. You Can See Daylight Through the Attic

Go into your attic on a sunny day and look up. If you can see light coming through the roof boards, water can get in too. This usually means the decking underneath the shingles has deteriorated and needs attention fast.

5. Water Stains on Your Ceilings or Walls

Brown stains on your ceiling or walls are a clear sign that water is getting past your roof. Sometimes a single leak can be traced to a damaged flashing or vent boot. But if you are seeing stains in multiple areas, the roof itself is likely failing.

6. Your Roof Is Sagging

A sagging roofline is a serious structural issue. It usually means the decking underneath has been weakened by moisture over time. If your roof is visibly sagging, do not wait. call a contractor for an inspection right away.

7. Storm Damage You Can See From the Ground

After a major hail storm or high-wind event, look for dents in your shingles, exposed underlayment, or debris scattered in your yard. Central Iowa sees severe storms every year, and even one bad hail event can take years off your roof's life. If you suspect storm damage, get a free inspection before filing an insurance claim.

How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Central Iowa?

In 2026, most residential roof replacements in the Ankeny and Des Moines metro area cost between $8,000 and $15,000. Here is what drives that number:

  • Roof size: Measured in "squares" (100 sq ft each). An average home is 20 to 30 squares.
  • Pitch (steepness): Steeper roofs require more safety equipment and labor time.
  • Material choice: Basic 3-tab shingles are the cheapest. Architectural shingles cost more but last longer. Metal roofing is a premium option that can push costs higher.
  • Tear-off layers: If old shingles need to be torn off (instead of overlaying), that adds labor and disposal costs.
  • Decking repairs: If the plywood underneath is rotted or damaged, it needs replaced before new shingles go on.

Will Insurance Cover My Roof Replacement?

If your roof was damaged by a covered event. hail, wind, fallen tree. your homeowners insurance will typically pay for the replacement minus your deductible. The key is having proper documentation.

Repair vs. Replace: How to Decide

Not every roof issue means you need a full replacement. Here is a simple way to think about it:

  • Repair if the damage is in one area, the rest of the roof is in good shape, and the roof is under 15 years old.
  • Replace if the roof is over 20 years old, has widespread damage, or you are making repairs every year or two.

A good contractor will give you an honest recommendation. At Black Ridge, we never push a full replacement when a simple repair will do the job. We want to earn your trust for the long haul. not just one project.
