10 Bathroom Remodel Ideas That Add Value to Your Iowa Home

Published April 6, 2026 • By Black Ridge Contracting

A bathroom remodel is one of the smartest investments you can make in your Iowa home. It does not take a massive budget to create a space that feels modern, functions better, and adds real value at resale. Whether you are updating a guest bath or transforming your master suite, these ten ideas will help you get the most out of your remodel.

1. Walk-In Shower

Ditching the old tub-shower combo for a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades we do in the Des Moines metro. A frameless glass enclosure with large-format tile makes even a small bathroom feel bigger. Add a built-in bench and a handheld showerhead for both style and function. Walk-in showers appeal to buyers of all ages, making them a strong value play at resale.

2. Double Vanity

If your bathroom has the space, a double vanity is a game-changer. No more fighting for counter space in the morning. Choose a floating vanity for a modern look or a furniture-style piece for something more traditional. Pair it with undermount sinks and quality faucets for a finished, polished feel.

3. Heated Floors

Iowa winters make heated bathroom floors more than a luxury. they are a practical upgrade. Electric radiant floor heating is installed under your tile and costs $6 to $12 per square foot. Once you step onto a warm floor on a cold January morning, you will wonder how you ever lived without it. The operating cost is only a few dollars per month.

4. Updated Tile Work

Tile sets the tone for your entire bathroom. Large-format porcelain tiles in neutral tones are trending in 2026 and create a clean, spa-like feel. Use accent tile in the shower niche or as a feature wall to add visual interest without going overboard. Quality tile work lasts decades and never goes out of style.

5. Modern Lighting

Good lighting transforms a bathroom. Replace that single overhead fixture with layered lighting. vanity sconces on either side of the mirror, recessed ceiling lights, and maybe a dimmer switch for evening relaxation. LED fixtures save energy and last for years. The right lighting makes your tile, counters, and fixtures look their best.

6. Smart Storage Solutions

Clutter kills a bathroom's appeal. Built-in niches in the shower, a medicine cabinet recessed into the wall, drawer organizers in the vanity, and open shelving for towels can all add storage without making the room feel cramped. Think about what you actually use in the bathroom every day and design storage around those items.

7. Accessibility Features

Whether you are planning to age in place or just want a safer bathroom, accessibility features are a smart addition. Grab bars near the toilet and in the shower, a curbless shower entry, comfort-height toilets, and non-slip tile are all upgrades that make the bathroom safer for everyone. These features also appeal to a wide range of buyers if you sell down the road.

8. Quality Fixtures and Hardware

Upgrading your faucets, showerhead, towel bars, and cabinet hardware is one of the most affordable ways to make a bathroom feel new. Choose a consistent finish. matte black, brushed nickel, and brushed gold are all popular in 2026. Quality fixtures not only look better, they last longer and function more smoothly.

9. Fresh Color Palette

A new color scheme can completely change the mood of your bathroom. Soft whites, warm grays, and earthy tones are all safe choices that feel clean and timeless. If you want to be bold, add color through accessories and towels that you can swap out easily. Keep the permanent surfaces. tile, counters, vanity. in neutral tones for maximum flexibility.

10. Proper Ventilation

This one is not glamorous, but it is critical. especially in Iowa where temperature swings create moisture problems. A quality exhaust fan keeps humidity under control and prevents mold, peeling paint, and moisture damage behind your walls. If your current fan sounds like a jet engine or barely moves air, replace it. Modern fans are whisper-quiet and much more effective.
