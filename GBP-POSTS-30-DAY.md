### Day 2
**Why Iowa roofs age faster than the rest of the country**
Iowa hits roofs harder than most states. We see 50 to 80 freeze-thaw cycles every winter, plus the summer hail. Most asphalt roofs in Central Iowa are working a 15 to 20 year service life, not the 30 year warranty number people quote. If your roof is past 15 years, worth getting eyes on it before the next storm season.

### Day 3
**The thing that kills concrete driveways in Iowa**
Iowa concrete fails for one main reason, road salt plus freeze-thaw. Salt eats the surface and then the freeze-thaw cycle pulls chunks off in spring. If your driveway is pitting or flaking, that's the early sign. Sealing in the first year buys you another decade.

*Button:* Learn more → /services/concrete

### Day 4
**Working on an older Beaverdale brick home this month**
The original mortar on these 1920s Beaverdale homes is lime-based, way softer than modern cement. After 80 to 100 years of Iowa freeze-thaw it starts crumbling at the joints. We always tuckpoint with a lime mortar match. Using portland cement on these old walls actually causes the brick face to spall. Beautiful homes, you just gotta work on them the right way.

**Older home renovation tip**

### Day 22
**Storm damage roof tip**
After a hail storm, do two things before calling your insurance. Take photos of any visible damage from the ground, and check the air conditioner condenser unit on the side of the house. If the AC fins are bent, that's documented impact velocity and helps your claim. Then call your insurance.

**Quick note on roof age**
If you bought your house in the last few years, your roof age is on the inspection report. Worth digging that out before storm season.
