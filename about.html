Our Story

Built on Hard Work, Handed Down With Pride

Back in 2020, my dad and I saw too many contractors in Central Iowa cutting corners. using cheap materials, dodging callbacks, leaving job sites a mess. We knew we could do better.

So we started Black Ridge Contracting out of Ankeny with nothing but a work ethic and a commitment to do things right. Dad brought 20+ years of hands-on construction experience. I brought the energy and the drive to build something that would last.

We spent those first years side-by-side. on roofs, pouring concrete, hanging siding in the Iowa heat. Every homeowner got the same treatment: show up when you say you will, do the work right the first time, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

Now that Dad has retired, he has handed the reins to me. And I carry that same standard into every single project. This is not just a business. it is our family name on the line. I hope to earn your trust the same way we have earned it from 200+ Central Iowa families before you.

What We Stand For

4 Values That Guide Every Project We Take On

These are not just words on a wall. They are how we run every job, every day.

Honest Communication

We tell you what needs done, what does not, and what it will cost. before any work starts. If we find something unexpected on your job, you hear about it immediately. No surprises, no runaround.

Quality Over Shortcuts

We use top-tier materials from trusted manufacturers. We do not cut corners on labor or materials because that is what creates callbacks. Do it right the first time. that is how Dad taught me and that is how we run this company.

Community First

We live here. We shop at the same stores, our kids go to the same schools. When you hire Black Ridge, you are hiring your neighbor. We take pride in making Central Iowa homes look their best.

Respect for Your Property

Your home is your biggest investment. Our crew treats it that way. covering landscaping, using drop cloths, cleaning up daily, and doing a final walkthrough with you before we leave. We are not done until you are happy.
