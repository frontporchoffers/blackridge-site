Serving West Des Moines, IA

Your Trusted Exterior Contractor in West Des Moines, Iowa

West Des Moines stretches from the historic charm of Valley Junction all the way out to the newer developments past Jordan Creek, and the contracting work changes dramatically depending on which part of the city you are in. Valley Junction is home to some of the oldest houses in West Des Moines. cottages and bungalows from the 1910s through the 1940s with real character and real maintenance needs. Roofing on these homes is often well past its lifespan, the original wood or aluminum siding is letting moisture creep behind the panels, and the gutters have been patched together so many times they barely function. We handle full exterior renovations in Valley Junction, replacing roofing, siding, and gutters in one coordinated project that protects the home and brings it back to life.

Over near Jordan Creek, the work looks completely different. These are larger homes built in the 2000s and 2010s, and the owners are focused on upgrading interiors that were finished with builder-grade materials. Kitchen remodeling is the number one request in this area. swapping out laminate countertops and stock cabinets for custom designs with solid surfaces and soft-close drawers. Bathroom remodeling runs a close second, with homeowners wanting spa-style master baths featuring modern tile work, frameless glass showers, and updated vanities. These homes look great from the curb, but the inside needs to match.

Mills Civic is the middle ground. These established 1990s neighborhoods have homes hitting the 25-to-30-year mark, which is when everything starts asking for attention at once. Roofing that has survived three decades of Iowa hail and wind needs a full tear-off, not just spot repairs. Siding is fading and becoming brittle. Concrete driveways and patios are cracking and heaving from decades of freeze-thaw punishment. We pour new concrete across West Des Moines every season, and we replace aging siding with fiber cement and engineered wood that holds up far better than what the original builder installed.

One thing that sets West Des Moines apart is HOA communities. Many neighborhoods here have strict architectural standards for materials, colors, and even gutter styles. We work within those guidelines every day and help homeowners choose options that satisfy the HOA while still looking the way they want. Whether it is a gutter replacement that needs to match specific color specs, basement finishing to add living space without a visible exterior change, or a full interior painting refresh to update a home that feels stuck in the 1990s, we handle the details so you do not end up fighting with your association.

Our crew drives in from Ankeny and can reach any part of West Des Moines in about 20 minutes. We bring honest pricing, clean job sites, and the same attention to detail whether we are working on a Valley Junction bungalow or a Jordan Creek executive home.

Roofing in West Des Moines

West Des Moines Roofing Contractor. Higher-End Homes, Same Honest Work.

West Des Moines has one of the widest housing stock spans in the metro. Older neighborhoods along Grand Avenue and Ashworth Road include 1950s and 1960s ranches with roofs that have been replaced two or three times already. Country Club Estates and Val Vista have mid-century split-levels and updated custom builds. The Bentonville corridor near Jordan Creek Town Center is packed with 2000s to 2020s new-build stock still on original shingles, most inside the 15-year mark. And the newest custom builds along Ashworth extension and the western edge of the city increasingly go straight to standing seam metal or Class 4 impact-resistant asphalt from day one.

Central Iowa storm exposure in West Des Moines is a bit less severe than the north metro (Ankeny, Johnston, and Grimes tend to sit in harder hail tracks), but hail and wind still hit here regularly. If your insurance premium keeps climbing, the biggest lever WDM homeowners have is upgrading to Class 4 impact-resistant shingles on the next replacement. Most Iowa insurers offer a real discount for that upgrade that often pays back the cost over the life of the roof. See our storm damage roof repair process if a recent storm hit your neighborhood.

West Des Moines homeowners tend to want higher-spec materials than the metro average. That means more standing seam metal roofs on newer customs (40 to 70-year lifespan), more architectural upgrades on asphalt replacements, and more attention to the finish detail on ridge caps, valleys, and flashing. Cost range: $10,000 to $18,000 for a standard asphalt install on a WDM home, with metal and specialty jobs running $20,000 to $40,000+ depending on square footage and complexity. Read the full roof replacement process for how we handle premium installs.

West Des Moines requires a building permit for roof replacement and requires the contractor to be registered with the city. We pull the permit and coordinate any required inspections. Many WDM neighborhoods also have HOA requirements around color, style, and material approvals. We prepare the HOA submission for you as part of the job so you do not have to manage that paperwork. If your roof is only partially damaged and repair makes more sense, see our roof repair services instead of jumping to a full replacement.

We work West Des Moines projects nearly every week. Licensed, insured, and focused on doing the job the right way the first time so we do not have to come back. Schedule a free inspection if you are not sure where your roof stands.
