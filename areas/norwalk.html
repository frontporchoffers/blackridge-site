Norwalk sits on the southern edge of the Des Moines metro, and its homes face the full range of Iowa weather. From hail damage on roofs to cracking concrete driveways, Black Ridge Contracting helps Norwalk homeowners protect and improve their properties with professional exterior services.

We handle your home inside and out: roofing, siding, gutters, concrete, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, basement finishing, and interior painting. Whether you are in the Lakewood area, the Echo Valley neighborhood, or one of the newer developments off Highway 28, Norwalk has a good mix of established homes and fresh construction. A lot of the homes built in the 1990s are now hitting the 25- to 30-year mark, and that is when everything seems to need attention at the same time. The roof that has been protecting your home for decades is past its expected lifespan. The siding is fading, cracking, or warping. The gutters are sagging or leaking at the seams. Instead of calling three different companies, Norwalk homeowners call us. because we handle roofing, siding, and gutters as one coordinated project. That saves you time, money, and headaches.

Inside those established Norwalk homes, the original finishes are starting to show their age too. Bathroom remodeling is right behind it, especially for those original 1990s bathrooms with builder-grade tile and basic vanities. A fresh bathroom with new tile, a modern vanity, and updated fixtures can change the way you start your day. Interior painting is another easy win that makes a big difference. a few coats of fresh paint brighten up rooms and give the whole house a cleaner feel.

Concrete is a big need in Norwalk too. The clay soil common in southern Polk County causes sidewalks and driveways to crack, settle, and shift over time. We remove the old broken concrete and pour new slabs that are properly reinforced for the local soil conditions. Basement finishing is popular in both the older and newer Norwalk homes, whether you want a space for the kids to play, a home office, or just extra storage with finished walls and flooring. Roofing in Norwalk

Norwalk's Roofing Contractor. Here's What Homeowners Actually Need to Know.

Norwalk sits southwest of the metro with a population around 13,000 and steady growth. Housing mix: older ranches and split-levels near Norwalk High School and the downtown area, plus significant 2010s to 2020s new build growth in Sunrise Pointe and along the North Ave corridor. Most Norwalk roofs are 2000s to 2020s with builder-grade asphalt shingles still in decent shape or approaching the 20-to-25-year replacement window on the earlier waves.

Central Iowa hail exposure in Norwalk is similar to West Des Moines. Slightly less severe than the north metro but hail still moves through regularly. Wind damage from spring and summer storms lifts shingles at ridges and eaves. See our storm damage roof repair process, or schedule a free inspection.

The typical Norwalk roof job runs $8,000 to $15,000 for a standard asphalt replacement. Larger custom builds in Sunrise Pointe or steeper pitches push higher. Norwalk requires a building permit for roof replacement and requires proper contractor licensing. We pull the permit for you. Read the full roof replacement process.

Sometimes a repair is the right call. Common Norwalk repair jobs include failed vent boots on 2000s builds, chimney flashing on older ranches, and missing shingles after wind. See the full list of roof repair jobs we handle.
