Basement finishing gives families extra living space without the cost of an addition, and interior painting is one of the fastest ways to make a 30-year-old home feel fresh again.

Roofing in Clive

Clive's Roofing Contractor. Here's What Homeowners Actually Need to Know.

Clive sits between West Des Moines and Urbandale with a population around 18,000 and mostly upscale established housing stock. Neighborhoods along NW 128th Street, near Country Club Glen, and the older core have 1970s to 1990s builds with roofs that have been through one or two replacement cycles already. Newer builds along the western edge and off Hickman Road have 2000s to 2020s asphalt roofs, most still in decent shape. Mature tree canopy through older Clive adds a wind-blown limb impact risk during severe storms.

Central Iowa hail exposure in Clive is real but slightly less severe than the northeast metro. Still, hail moves through the city regularly and wind damage from spring and summer storms lifts shingles at the ridge, eaves, and around penetrations. See our storm damage roof repair process, or schedule a free inspection.

Clive homeowners often opt for higher-spec materials than the metro average. Cost range: $10,000 to $18,000 for standard asphalt install, with standing seam metal and premium jobs on custom homes running $20,000 to $40,000+. Class 4 impact-resistant shingles are worth pricing given Iowa hail patterns and the homeowners insurance discount most Iowa carriers offer for that upgrade. Read the full roof replacement process.

Sometimes a repair is the right call, especially on Clive homes with roofs under 15 years old and localized damage. Failed vent boots, chimney flashing, and missing shingles after wind are common Clive repairs. See the full list of roof repair jobs we handle. We do not push replacements when a repair will do the job.

Clive requires a building permit for roof replacement and requires proper contractor licensing. We pull the permit and coordinate any required inspections. Some Clive neighborhoods have HOA requirements around color and material approvals, which we handle on your behalf.
