Nearly half of Ankeny's homes were built in 2010 or later, and the median home dates to 2008. Bathrooms in houses that age usually have modern plumbing, an exhaust fan and room to move. What often wears out first is the builder's fiberglass tub and shower unit, the vanity top and the single light over the mirror.

So an Ankeny bathroom remodel tends to be a targeted upgrade. The pipes mostly stay where they are, which keeps the cost down, and the money goes into tile, glass and cabinetry.

The Work

Common upgrades in newer Ankeny bathrooms

Tub-to-shower conversions

In a primary bath, a tub that rarely gets used can come out for a walk-in tiled shower. The drain usually has to move a little, and that takes a plumbing permit.

Tiled showers that stay dry

The shower pan and walls get a continuous waterproof layer before any tile goes on.

Double vanities and storage

A longer vanity with two sinks, drawers in place of doors and a linen tower make a shared bathroom easier to use.

Hall and kids' bathrooms

A one-piece fiberglass tub and surround can be replaced with a new tub and tile walls without rebuilding the whole room.

Lighting, fans and warm floors

Quieter exhaust fans, better mirror lighting and electric floor heat are wired by a licensed electrician on circuits sized for them.

A bathroom in the basement

Newer homes were often built with drain lines roughed in under the basement floor for a future bathroom. If yours has them, adding a bath downstairs costs far less. See our Ankeny basement finishing page.

Permits

Bathroom permits in Ankeny

Plumbing, electrical and mechanical work in Ankeny each need a permit, and the city requires a contractor licensed in that trade to pull it and do the work. Details are on the City of Ankeny building information page.

For a basement bathroom, the city's guidelines call for an exhaust fan vented to the outside and a dedicated 20-amp, ground-fault protected outlet. Plan review for a house runs about five working days at the city, and an inspector needs two days' notice. The inspection line is 515-963-3533.

Cost

What a bathroom remodel costs in Ankeny

Ankeny is not one of the report's markets, so the Des Moines numbers are the nearest guide. According to the Remodeling 2025 Cost vs. Value Report (www.costvsvalue.com), a midrange bathroom remodel there averages $24,330 and adds about $14,231 at resale. It covers a 5-by-7-foot bathroom with every fixture replaced.

An upscale remodel that expands a 35-square-foot bathroom to 100 square feet and moves all the fixtures averages $77,292 and adds about $26,778.

A tub-to-shower conversion or a vanity and flooring update is a smaller job than either of those. We price your actual scope in writing after a visit.
