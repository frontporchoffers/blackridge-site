Roof Repair Basics

When a Roof Repair Is the Right Call

A roof repair in Central Iowa is usually the right call when the damage is localized to a small area, the roof is under about 15 years old, and the rest of the shingles still have life in them. Common repairs we handle include leak repair around vents and flashing, replacing a handful of missing or curling shingles, fixing chimney flashing, resealing pipe boots, and patching small storm damage. A repair typically runs $350 to $1,500 in the Des Moines metro, takes a half day to a full day on-site, and buys you years of remaining roof life without the cost of a full replacement. If your roof is over 20 years old or the damage is widespread, a full tear-off usually makes more financial sense long-term. We tell you honestly which way to go.

Common Repairs

Roof Repairs We Handle Every Week in Central Iowa

💧

Roof Leak Repair

We track down where the water is actually getting in, which is often not directly above the ceiling stain. Fix the source, check the surrounding area, and stop the leak for good.

🏠

Missing or Curling Shingles

Iowa wind and age lift shingles off the roof one at a time. We replace what is gone or damaged and inspect the surrounding rows for compromised seals.

🔣

Flashing Repair

Failed flashing around chimneys, skylights, dormers, and roof-to-wall joints is one of the top sources of leaks we see. We remove, reseal, and reinstall to a proper watertight finish.

📌

Vent Boot Replacement

Rubber vent boots crack after 10 to 15 years in Iowa sun and freeze cycles. Replacing them is one of the cheapest and highest-return roof repairs a homeowner can make.

🔥

Chimney Flashing

Chimney flashing is one of the most common leak sources on older Central Iowa homes. We handle step flashing, counter flashing, and full rebuilds where needed.

🛠

Small Storm Damage

Not every storm requires a full replacement. If a few shingles blew off or a branch punched a small hole, we make it right without pushing you toward a bigger job.

Our Repair Process

Honest Inspection First, Repair Second

The way most roof repairs go wrong is that someone quotes the fix without really knowing what caused the problem. That is how a leak comes back six months later in a new spot.

  • Free inspection: We walk the roof, take photos, and find the actual source of the problem.
  • Written quote: You get a firm price with materials, labor, and timeline before any work starts.
  • Root-cause fix: We repair the source of the problem, not just the visible damage.
  • Surrounding check: We check adjacent shingles, seals, and flashing so a fix in one spot does not leave a new problem next to it.
  • Cleanup: Job site cleaned every day, magnets across the yard when we finish.

If we walk the roof and find the honest answer is a full replacement instead of a repair, we tell you that too. No games.
