Our Process

How Our Interior Painting Process Works

We keep things simple and straightforward so you always know what to expect from your interior painting project.

🎨

1. Color Consultation

We meet at your home, talk through your vision, and help you choose the right colors and finishes for every room. We bring samples so you can see how colors look in your own space with your lighting.

🛠

2. Prep and Protection

We move furniture, lay drop cloths, tape edges, and repair any drywall damage. Every surface gets cleaned and primed where needed. This step is what separates a professional interior painting job from a DIY attempt.

🔨

3. Paint Application

Our painters apply two coats using brushes and rollers for a smooth, even finish. We cut in tight edges by hand and roll large surfaces for consistent coverage. Every wall, ceiling, and trim piece gets the attention it deserves.

✅

4. Final Touch-Up

Before we call a project done, we inspect every surface under bright light. We touch up any spots, remove all tape and coverings, put furniture back, and clean up completely. You get a walkthrough to make sure everything looks perfect.
