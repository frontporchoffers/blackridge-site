What We Do

What We Do in Your Kitchen

Whether you want a simple refresh or a complete kitchen remodel, our crew handles every part of the job. No subcontractors, no runaround. just skilled work from a team that takes pride in every detail.

🚪

Cabinet Installation & Replacement

New cabinets change everything about a kitchen. We install stock, semi-custom, and custom cabinets to fit your space and style. Whether you want a full swap or a cabinet refacing, we handle it from start to finish.

🪨

Countertop Installation

Granite, quartz, butcher block, or laminate. we help you pick the right surface and install it with precision. New countertops are one of the fastest ways to transform the look and feel of your kitchen in Des Moines or Ankeny.

🧱

Kitchen Flooring

We install tile, luxury vinyl plank, hardwood, and other flooring options built to handle the daily wear and tear of a busy kitchen. Our team makes sure the subfloor is prepped right so your new floors last for years.

🎨

Backsplash & Tile Work

A well-done backsplash ties the whole kitchen together. Subway tile, mosaic, natural stone. whatever your style, we lay it clean and level. It is one of those details that makes your kitchen remodel look complete.

📏

Layout Changes & Islands

Want to open up your kitchen or add an island for extra prep space? We handle layout changes including wall removal, plumbing relocation, and island construction. Our team builds kitchens that work the way you cook and live.

💡

Lighting & Electrical Updates

Good lighting makes a kitchen feel bigger, brighter, and more functional. We install recessed lighting, under-cabinet LEDs, pendant lights, and updated outlets. If your Central Iowa kitchen feels dark and outdated, new lighting changes everything.

Common Questions

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs. Answered by Our Team

Got questions about your kitchen remodel? Here are the answers to the ones we hear most from homeowners across Des Moines and Central Iowa.

Most kitchen remodels in the Des Moines metro area cost between $15,000 and $50,000 depending on the scope of work. A basic refresh with new countertops and cabinet refacing will be on the lower end, while a full gut renovation with layout changes, custom cabinets, and premium finishes will be closer to the top of that range. We provide free in-home estimates so you know the exact cost before work begins.

A typical kitchen remodel in Central Iowa takes 4 to 8 weeks from start to finish. Simple updates like countertop and cabinet replacements can be done in 3 to 4 weeks, while full renovations involving plumbing, electrical, and layout changes may take 8 weeks or more. We give you a clear timeline before we start so you can plan around it.

Yes, many kitchen updates can be done without moving plumbing at all. Replacing cabinets, countertops, flooring, backsplash, and lighting can completely transform your kitchen while keeping the sink and dishwasher in their current locations. This approach saves time and money. We will help you figure out the best plan for your goals and budget.

A full kitchen remodel typically includes new cabinets, countertops, flooring, backsplash, lighting, and an updated appliance layout. It may also include plumbing and electrical work, adding or removing walls, installing a kitchen island, and updating fixtures. We handle every detail from design to final cleanup so you get a complete transformation.

Yes, permits are typically required for kitchen remodeling work that involves electrical changes, plumbing modifications, or structural alterations. Cosmetic updates like painting, new countertops, or cabinet replacement usually do not require permits. We handle all permit applications and inspections so you do not have to worry about it.

We can discuss flexible payment options to help make your kitchen remodel affordable. During your free estimate, we will walk through the project cost and talk about payment plans that work for your budget. Our goal is to help you get the kitchen you want without unnecessary financial stress.
