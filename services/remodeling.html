Why One Contractor

One Contractor for Your Entire Home

Most homeowners in Des Moines and Central Iowa end up hiring three, four, even five different contractors for a single remodel. one for drywall, another for flooring, someone else for trim, and a painter on top of that. Every handoff creates delays, miscommunication, and finger-pointing when something goes wrong.

With Black Ridge Contracting as your general contractor, you get one team, one point of contact, and one schedule. We coordinate every trade and every phase so your home remodeling project stays on track and on budget. That means fewer headaches, faster timelines, and better results.

  • Single Point of Contact: You talk to us. We manage every subcontractor, supplier, and detail so you do not have to.
  • No Scheduling Gaps: When one phase ends, the next one starts. No waiting weeks for the next trade to show up.
  • Consistent Quality: Our standards apply to every phase. drywall, flooring, trim, painting, and finishing. Nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Cost Savings: Bundling services with one general contractor eliminates duplicate setup fees, reduces material waste, and keeps your total home remodeling cost lower.
  • Accountability: If anything needs fixed, there is no question about who handles it. We own the entire project.
