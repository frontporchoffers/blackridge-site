Built for Iowa

Why Iowa Concrete Needs Expert Care

Central Iowa is tough on concrete. Temperatures can swing from 95 degrees in July to negative 20 in January. That kind of freeze-thaw cycle puts enormous stress on any slab. and cheap concrete work shows it fast.

When water seeps into small cracks and freezes, it expands and breaks the concrete apart from the inside. That is why proper installation matters so much in our climate. At Black Ridge, we build every concrete project to handle what Iowa throws at it.

  • ✓ Rebar and wire mesh reinforcement on every pour
  • ✓ Expansion joints to allow natural movement
  • ✓ Proper grading and compacted base for drainage
  • ✓ Control joints cut at the right spacing to manage cracking
  • ✓ Quality sealer applied to protect against moisture and salt

Cutting corners on any of these steps leads to cracking, settling, and early replacement. We do it right the first time so your concrete lasts.

Removal & Prep
