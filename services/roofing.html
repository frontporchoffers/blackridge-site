Materials

Roofing Materials We Install

We carry a full range of roofing materials to match your home's style, your budget, and Iowa's tough weather conditions.

Asphalt Shingles

The most popular roofing choice in Ankeny and Central Iowa. Available in architectural and designer styles with 25 to 50-year warranties. Affordable, durable, and great-looking on any home.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam metal roofs last 40 to 70 years and handle Iowa's hail, wind, and snow like nothing else. Energy efficient, low maintenance, and available in a wide range of colors.

Rubber Roofing (EPDM)

The go-to solution for flat and low-slope roofs. EPDM rubber roofing is waterproof, UV-resistant, and built to handle the temperature swings Central Iowa throws at it. Great for garages, additions, and commercial buildings.
