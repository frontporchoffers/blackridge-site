Privacy

This page says what this website collects, who receives it and how to opt out. Last updated November 20, 2026.

What you send us

When you request an estimate through a form on this site, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address, the service you need and your project details. The form is handled by a third-party form service, which delivers it to our email. We use it to contact you about your request. We do not sell it.

What the site collects automatically

This site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. Both are tools from other companies, Google and Meta. They use cookies and similar technologies to collect or receive information about your visit, such as your IP address, the pages you view, what you click, the time of your visit, and the device and browser you use.

We use that information to measure how the site is used and to show our ads to people who have visited it. Google and Meta may also collect information from other websites and apps you use, and use it to provide measurement services and to target ads.

Google explains its side at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses and the pages requested.

Your choices

  • Opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Change the ads Meta shows you in the ad preferences of your Facebook or Instagram account.
  • Block Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.
